# C4 versus C3: descriptive change summary

The common core was held fixed. Only optimized branch deltas differed. This document contains no optimized prompt text or patch hunks.

| Branch | C3 lines | C4 lines | Added lines | Removed lines | Descriptive change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| career | 7 | 162 | 162 | 7 | Expanded elicitation of qualifications, education, work gaps and feasible work conditions; reinforced profile reflection and practical alternatives. |
| outplacement | 5 | 170 | 169 | 4 | Expanded transferable-skill and retirement-context exploration; added emotional acknowledgement and concrete transition planning. |
| hope_return | 5 | 150 | 150 | 5 | Clarified closure stage and re-employment versus restart goals; avoided repeated referral advice and strengthened grounded alternatives. |
| local_gov | 5 | 151 | 151 | 5 | Expanded local constraints, travel range, qualifications and previous search history; reduced repeated referral lists and emphasized local policy/tool use. |
| university | 7 | 106 | 104 | 5 | Expanded study-stage, experience and practical constraints; emphasized contextual empathy and actionable entry-level preparation. |

GEPA exceeded its configured evaluation budget (about 1.4×) and was manually stopped; a Pareto candidate was finalized. Exploration rules may be learnable by the evaluation metric. A held-out judge supported the same direction but cannot establish absence of metric gaming. C4 raw prompts and optimizer execution code are excluded. Therefore this release reproduces reported statistics, not C4 generation or optimization.
